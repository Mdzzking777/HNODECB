State notions
-------------

x1:
  Tip displacement.
  Unit: m.

x2:
  Tip velocity, equal to x1dot.
  Unit: m/s.

x3:
  Sample surface / Kelvin-Voigt deformation.
  Unit: m.

x2dot:
  Tip acceleration, computed directly from the ODE.
  Unit: m/s^2.

x3dot:
  Sample deformation velocity, computed directly from the explicit DMT-KV closure.
  Unit: m/s.

s:
  Tip-sample distance.
  Formula: s = dist + x1 - x3.
  Unit: m.

contact:
  Contact indicator.
  Formula: contact = 1 if s <= a0, otherwise 0.

fts:
  Tip-sample interaction force.
  Unit: N.

Mechanistic parameters
----------------------

These are the mechanistic parameters estimated in AFM04. They are not NN/KAN parameters.

ks true:
  Value: 0.1
  Unit: N/m
  Meaning: Kelvin-Voigt sample stiffness.

cs true:
  Value: 2.4e-7
  Unit: N*s/m
  Meaning: Kelvin-Voigt sample damping.

st1pl grid range:

ks range:
  [0.005, 5.0] N/m

cs range:
  [5.0e-8, 5.0e-6] N*s/m

Force law and state-space equation
----------------------------------

Distance:

  s = dist + x1 - x3

Smooth transition:

  g(s) = sigmoid(beta * (s - a0))

Indentation:

  delta = max(a0 - s, 0)

Adhesion term:

  adhesion = -A*R / (6 * max(g(s)*(s-a0) + a0, 1e-15)^2)

Hertz term:

  f_hertz = (4/3) * Estar * sqrt(R) * delta^(3/2)

Kelvin-Voigt coefficient:

  kv_coeff = eta_star * sqrt(R) * sqrt(delta)

Static force:

  f_static = adhesion + (1 - g) * f_hertz

Explicit x3dot closure:

  x3dot = (-f_static + kv_coeff*x2 - ks*x3) / (cs + kv_coeff)

Indentation rate:

  delta_dot = x3dot - x2, if delta > 0
  delta_dot = 0,          otherwise

Full tip-sample force:

  Fts = f_static + kv_coeff * delta_dot

State-space equation:

  x1dot = x2

  x2dot = (Fd*cos(wd*t) - k*x1 - c*x2 + Fts) / m

  x3dot = (-f_static + kv_coeff*x2 - ks*x3) / (cs + kv_coeff)

Data generation notes
---------------------

1. The synthetic trajectory is noise-free for e0.0.

2. x2dot, x3dot, delta_dot, and Fts are not obtained by numerical differentiation.
   They are computed directly from the ODE / force-law closure.

3. The synthetic trajectory is sampled every 16 ns, so the sampling frequency is 62.5 MHz.

4. ode_data only stores the three state variables x1, x2, x3.

5. pert_df stores auxiliary observables and diagnostics:
   x2dot, contact, s, x3dot, delta_dot, and fts.

6. AFM04 formal W0 training does not begin at full trajectory index 0.
   It begins after truncation at first contact.
