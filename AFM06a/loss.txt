AFM06a Loss and Physics-Informed Definition
===========================================

Purpose
-------

AFM06a learns the unknown effective tip-sample interaction force from the
known cantilever dynamics. The optimization objective is evaluated on the
sampled true x1, x2, and x2dot values; a rollout is retained as a diagnostic
and is not the quantity minimized by the optimizer.

KAN target
----------

The single-input 1-3-1 KAN receives normalized x1 and predicts the effective
interaction acceleration

    bar_F_ts_pred = KAN(x1_norm).

Gain and Soft Mask are disabled. Therefore the KAN output is used directly in
the governing equation.

Governing equation
------------------

For the hard-sample system,

    x1dot = x2,

    x2dot
    =
    A_act sin(omega0 t)
    - omega0^2 x1
    - D(s) omega0 x2
    + bar_F_ts,

where D(s) is the known distance-dependent damping coefficient.

Dynamics residual
-----------------

At every sampled true state, the predicted force is inserted into the known
right-hand side:

    r_dyn(t_i)
    =
    x2dot_true(t_i)
    - [
        A_act sin(omega0 t_i)
        - omega0^2 x1_true(t_i)
        - D(s_true(t_i)) omega0 x2_true(t_i)
        + bar_F_ts_pred(t_i)
      ].

The only learned quantity in this expression is bar_F_ts_pred.

Normalized dynamics-residual loss
---------------------------------

For a selected train or validation index set I, define

    A_x2dot
    =
    sqrt[
        (1 / |I|) sum_(i in I) x2dot_true(t_i)^2
    ].

Then

    L_dyn
    =
    (1 / |I|) sum_(i in I)
    [
        r_dyn(t_i) / A_x2dot
    ]^2.

Its target is exactly zero for the noiseless synthetic system.

Residual-smoothness loss
------------------------

Within each uninterrupted contact or noncontact segment, the normalized
residual curvature is evaluated on the nonuniform sampled time grid. For an
interior point i,

    kappa_i
    =
    2
    [
        (r_(i+1) - r_i) / h_right
        - (r_i - r_(i-1)) / h_left
    ]
    /
    (h_left + h_right),

where r is the normalized dynamics residual. The dimensionless local
curvature contribution is

    kappa_i [0.5 (h_left + h_right)]^2.

The smoothness term is the mean square of these contributions:

    L_sm
    =
    mean_i
    {
        kappa_i [0.5 (h_left + h_right)]^2
    }^2.

Triplets crossing a contact transition are excluded so the known physical
regime switch is not penalized as artificial roughness.

Final loss
----------

    L_total = L_dyn + lambda_sm L_sm,

with

    lambda_sm = 1.

The saved policy name is

    force_scaled_dynamics_residual_true_x1_residual_smooth

Why this is physics-informed
----------------------------

The KAN is not trained against force labels. Its output is judged by whether
it satisfies the known cantilever governing equation at the observed states.
The residual-smoothness term regularizes oscillatory residual errors without
replacing the governing-equation objective.

The differentiable rollout is used for trajectory reconstruction diagnostics,
entry replay, and final reporting. The Adam and L-BFGS optimization closures
minimize the pointwise physics residual and residual-smoothness terms.
